Understand how the suggested allocation is calculated.

ALLOCATION METHOD

Revenue-Based Allocation

The optimizer distributes the existing campaign budget according to each campaign's share of recorded revenue. When all campaign revenues are zero, it uses an equal allocation as a fallback.

These are rule-based planning suggestions, not guaranteed financial or marketing outcomes. Review the recommendations before making budget decisions.
